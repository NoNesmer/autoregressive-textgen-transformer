double, double toil and trouble the earth,
and then the son of the king that suffers him
to suffer him henry the french king and son.

king henry.
say, what a thousand times he would be thus?

warwick.
nay, my lord, i will call thee resolve thee.

king henry.
thy life is ready in the tower.

plantagenet.
the king hath lost him and made him for thee.

king henry.
on the county of thy soul i know not what i prithee?

suffolk.
i am a good sovereign of thy life.

king henry.
and how many good morrows have you heard
