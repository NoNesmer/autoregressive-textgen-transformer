o romeo, romeo! wherefore art thou romeo?

romeo.
ay, that thou dost love me, and with thy knee
i do not speak to make my tongue.

juliet.
i will be bold to love thee, and be my father.

romeo.
and thou art the love to think on me.

juliet.
this was not the good day to say i am not yet.

romeo.
what is thy name?

juliet.
i know not.

juliet.
then i will be thy comfort.

romeo.
then was i think thou hast mine own country.

juliet.
why, thou art a fair song.

romeo.
and therefore be my power to my father.

juliet.
i will not but the same more than i
